MapleCW Drop Tables

Every monster's drops on this server, with the chance at the server's current drop rate. Chances follow the v83 tables where the monster has one. A monster's own drops scale with the rate and stop at 100%. The global drops never scale.

The Companion's Magic Box from First Time Together is listed with the monsters: its "1 in N" counts boxes, not kills.

"1 in N" is the average number of kills per drop, rounded. Expected drops per kill add up the chances of every equipment or scroll row. Rows marked quest drop only for a player who has that quest in progress. Mesos marked by level are the server's default for a monster with no meso row: always dropped, and not affected by the drop rate.

No source has Nexon's official per-monster rates. The v83 numbers come from Cosmic, the maintained OdinMS/HeavenMS server, and are the closest classic calibration there is.
