Only monsters you can meet are listed. Found on names every map that spawns the monster, with how many spawn points it has there, most first. A monster that only appears another way, such as the ship invasion or a summoning sack, says so.

"1 in N" is the average number of kills per drop, rounded. Expected drops per kill add up the chances of every equipment or scroll row. Rows marked quest drop only for a player who has that quest in progress. Mesos marked by level are the server's default for a monster with no meso row: always dropped, and not affected by the drop rate.

Seen counts what actually dropped on this server over the last 7 days, with the share of kills it dropped from. The counts update at most every 30 minutes, and anything older than 7 days is deleted.
