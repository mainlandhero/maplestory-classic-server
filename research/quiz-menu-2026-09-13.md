## 3. What the server does now

* `Quest::say_indices` keeps the WZ index of every line beside `Quest::say`, so
  `1.stop.0`'s three lines are known to be `0, 1, 3` and not positions `0, 1, 2`.
* `say_line(0)` on a path whose node has `ask` sends `npc_menu` (type 6) instead of a Say.
* `quiz_menu_answer` claims the type-6 reply while the conversation is parked on a question
  (same precondition shape as the taxi's and the Administrator's): closed -> nothing; the
  right choice -> `say_line(1)`; a wrong one -> its `stop.0.<n>` line as a Say with Next, on
  path `<path>.quiz.retry`, and the Next re-asks the question. A choice with no stop line
  re-asks straight away.
