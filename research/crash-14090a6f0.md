## What the faulting instruction is

`tools/pdata_lookup.py` puts it at `+0x250` into `FUN_14090a4a0` (`0x14090a4a0..0x14090a8f2`,
1106 bytes). The listing:

```text
14090a6e9  inc      r15d
14090a6ec  mov      rax, qword ptr [rbx + 0x10]
14090a6f0  cmp      byte ptr [rax + 0x19], 0     <- faults
14090a6f4  je       0x14090a718
14090a6f6  mov      rax, qword ptr [rbx + 8]
14090a6fa  cmp      byte ptr [rax + 0x19], 0
14090a6fe  jne      0x14090a713
```

**This is an MSVC `std::map` walk.** `_Tree_node` is `{ _Left, _Parent, _Right, _Myval...,
_Color, _Isnil }`; `+0x08` and `+0x10` are two of the three child pointers and `+0x19` is
`_Isnil`. Reading both children and testing `_Isnil` on each is the standard successor/insert
descent. So the fault is **a bad node pointer while walking a tree**, with a counter (`r15d`)
being incremented per iteration.

That is consistent with a corrupted or concurrently-mutated map. It is **also** consistent
with several other things, and one dump cannot tell them apart - `CLAUDE.md` records a
retraction that came from reading exactly this much into one crash.
